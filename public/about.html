A connected resilience system

From risk intelligence to decisions—and from decisions to resilience outcomes.

DDRiVE-M is a data-driven resilience management platform by ASilva Innovations. It is designed to help local governments, public institutions, enterprises and civil-society organisations connect risk information, governance, operational action and learning in one continuous cycle.

The organising architecture

Four dimensions. One connected system.

The value of resilience intelligence comes from the connections between evidence, authority, action and results—not from isolated dashboards.

01

Risk intelligence

Bring together attributed warnings, reported hazards, risk records and assessment evidence. Make source, time, uncertainty and data gaps visible.

02

Governance

Clarify mandates, roles, accountability, plans and institutional capability. Connect priorities to responsible offices and evidence.

03

Operational decisions

Support coordination, treatment, anticipatory-action workflows, exercises and documented human-authorised decisions.

04

Resilience outcomes

Monitor progress, validate what works, capture lessons and use results to improve the next planning and operating cycle.

The integrated resilience intelligence cycle

Seven phases, continuously connected

Each phase informs the next. Monitoring and learning feed back into detection, diagnosis and better decisions—so resilience becomes a managed process, not a one-time assessment.

PHASE 01

Detection

Bring official warning information and organisation-reported observations into a shared operational picture. Preserve source, issue time, expiry and freshness.

Available
PHASE 02

Diagnosis

Organise risks and identify priorities. The current risk register is a foundation—not a completed CDRA.

Partly available
PHASE 03

Response

Translate risk and incident information into assigned treatments and coordinated action. The RA 12287 workflow records its process while statutory decisions remain with competent authorities.

Partly available
PHASE 04

Integration

Connect priorities with institutional governance and the UNDRR Ten Essentials, linking evidence to plans, owners and budgets.

Partly available
PHASE 05

Validation

Test plans, people and coordination through scenarios and exercises; record findings and corrective actions.

Partly available
PHASE 06

Enhancement

Turn lessons and assessment findings into improved DRRM, continuity, adaptation and recovery planning. Outputs remain drafts until approved.

Partly available
PHASE 07

Monitoring

Track operational updates and progress, then feed results and lessons into the next cycle.

Partly available

The cycle is iterative: new evidence, changing conditions and lessons learned should continuously update priorities and action.

Current capability and maturity

Clear about what is available today

DDRiVE-M is in pre-production development. The status labels below distinguish implemented platform surfaces from capabilities that are partial or still being built.

AVAILABLE

Official PAGASA alert ingestion

Official PAGASA alert information is ingested with source and time context for organisation watch areas. DDRiVE-M relays information; it does not originate or replace official warnings.

AVAILABLE

RA 12287 workflow

The anticipatory-action workspace supports the recorded workflow, including assessment, recommendation, declaration, measures and funds, lifting and reporting. Competent authorities retain statutory decision-making.

PARTLY AVAILABLE

Risk diagnosis and resilience integration

The platform includes a risk register and UNDRR Ten Essentials scorecard surfaces. Full hazard–exposure–vulnerability–capacity analysis and evidence-backed indicators remain development priorities.

PARTLY AVAILABLE

Response, validation and monitoring

Incident tracking, exercise scenarios, plan drafting and team updates provide a foundation. End-to-end EOC workflows, harmonised reporting and outcome monitoring are not yet complete.

Important: DDRiVER AI is not connected yet. Do not rely on an AI assistant for operational advice. The PWA shell may load offline, but live records, alerts and authority actions require connectivity.
