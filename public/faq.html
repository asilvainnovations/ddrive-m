Getting started

What is DDRiVE-M?

DDRiVE-M (Data-Driven Resilience Management) is a resilience intelligence platform by ASilva Innovations. It is designed to connect risk intelligence, governance, operational decisions and resilience outcomes through a seven-phase cycle. It supports organisational work; it does not replace official warning authorities, statutory decision-makers or approved plans and procedures.

Who is the platform designed for?

The initial audience includes local governments and DRRM offices, public institutions, enterprises concerned with continuity, NGOs, consultants and resilience partners. Suitability depends on the organisation’s workflows, data readiness, connectivity, governance and operating capacity.

Is DDRiVE-M production-ready?

No. The repository describes DDRiVE-M as pre-production. Some capabilities are available, some are partial, and others remain in development. An organisation should not treat the platform as a fully validated operational command system or as a certified compliance solution.

The seven-phase intelligence cycle

What are the seven phases?

The cycle comprises: (1) Detection, (2) Diagnosis, (3) Response, (4) Integration, (5) Validation, (6) Enhancement and (7) Monitoring. It is iterative: monitoring, exercise findings and operational lessons should inform the next round of detection, diagnosis and decisions.

How does DDRiVE-M detect hazards?

The current codebase includes ingestion of official PAGASA alert information, with source and time context for organisation watch areas. The platform can also hold hazard records logged from an alert. Do not assume PHIVOLCS, NOAH, GeoRiskPH or other multi-agency feeds are already integrated. Follow PAGASA, PHIVOLCS and the local DRRM office for official information.

Does DDRiVE-M provide complete climate and disaster risk assessment?

Not yet. The current diagnosis module includes a risk register based on likelihood and impact. A full CDRA requires evidence on hazards, exposure, vulnerability, capacity, climate conditions, spatial context and affected populations. The current risk register should not be represented as a completed CDRA or a substitute for locally validated assessment.

What response functions are currently available?

The repository includes an incident list and progress tracking, plus a separate anticipatory-action workspace. End-to-end EOC activation, Incident Command System tasking, resource and logistics tracking, and harmonised situation reporting remain development priorities.

How does the platform support integration and resilience governance?

The Integration module includes UNDRR Ten Essentials scorecard entries and guidance. The intended direction is to connect indicators with evidence, baselines, accountable offices, targets, budgets, milestones and outcomes. Do not interpret a scorecard entry as institutional certification.

Can the platform validate plans and exercises?

The Validation module includes exercise scenarios and links to ICS simulators. Exercise design, participation, observations, after-action reviews and corrective-action closure should be treated as an evidence trail. The complete end-to-end validation workflow is still being developed.

Does DDRiVE-M automatically produce approved plans?

No. The Enhancement module includes plan drafts from templates. Any generated or assisted content is a draft and must be reviewed, adapted and approved by an authorised person before it becomes an official plan or procedure.

What does Monitoring currently do?

The Monitoring module includes team messages and presence. More complete outcome indicators, disaggregated reporting, data-quality checks and structured feedback into the next cycle remain development priorities.

Warnings, anticipatory action and authority

Does DDRiVE-M issue official warnings or evacuation orders?

No. DDRiVE-M relays and attributes official information; it does not originate official warnings, evacuation orders, State of Imminent Disaster declarations or other statutory directions. Always follow the competent government authorities and local DRRM office.

How does the RA 12287 workflow work?

The codebase includes an end-to-end workflow for recording forecast triggers, pre-disaster risk assessment, recommendation, declaration, measures and funds, lifting and reporting to OCD. The software records and supports the process. It does not make the statutory determination, issue the declaration or independently authorise public expenditure. Graduated disbursement rules are not represented as fully encoded.

Will DDRiVE-M automatically mobilise staff or release funds?

No. The platform should not be treated as an autonomous authority for staff mobilisation, declarations, evacuation, expenditure or fund release. These actions must follow applicable law, approved protocols and the decisions of authorised officials.

Can DDRiVE-M replace an EOC or a Manual of Operations?

No. It can support information organisation and coordination workflows, but institutional readiness also depends on an operational EOC, alternate arrangements, trained personnel, communications, supplies, SOPs, exercises and clear authority. The platform is not a substitute for those capabilities.

Data, privacy and responsible use

How is organisational data separated?

The application architecture uses organisation-scoped records and database access policies. The repository describes role-based access and tenant isolation as part of the design. A live deployment still requires environment-specific configuration, access-rule testing, security review and operational assurance. Do not infer independent security certification from the presence of access-control code.

Is all personal data encrypted and fully compliant with RA 10173?

This page does not make a blanket encryption or compliance guarantee. Organisations must establish lawful purpose and basis, controller/processor responsibilities, access rules, retention, deletion, data-sharing terms, privacy-impact assessment and incident procedures. Verify the live hosting, backup and subprocessor arrangements before processing personal or sensitive personal data.

Can the FPIC tracker store actual Indigenous Peoples’ consent records?

The repository describes the FPIC tracker as training/practice only until secure server-side storage and appropriate community-controlled governance exist. Do not use it for real household, community or FPIC records.

Does the platform work offline?

The installable application shell and some application assets can load offline. This does not mean live records, alerts, messaging, synchronisation or authority actions are available without connectivity. Treat any offline capability as limited unless a specific workflow has been tested and documented.

Is DDRiVER AI available?

Not yet. The current application marks the assistant as not connected. Do not rely on it for real-time guidance or operational decisions. Any future AI service should be grounded in approved sources, provide citations, log appropriately and keep human review and approval in the decision loop.

Plans, support and contact

How much does DDRiVE-M cost?

Published subscription rates and commercial feature tiers are not currently available. Enquiries can be made for an initial consultation, guided demonstration or scoped deployment discussion. Any commercial proposal will define fees, deliverables, responsibilities and support conditions.

How can our organisation request a demonstration?

Contact ASilva Innovations at info@asilvainnovations.com and briefly describe your organisation, intended users and the resilience workflow you want to explore. A demonstration should distinguish current capability from planned development.

Where can we learn more about the seven-phase model?

Start with the About page for the platform architecture and current capability status. For deployment or operational use, request a discussion about governance, institutional readiness, data management and acceptance requirements.
